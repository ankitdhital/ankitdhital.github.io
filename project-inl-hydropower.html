INL Hydropower-Battery Display

Our ME 490 capstone team of four mechanical engineering students is building a portable tabletop display for Idaho National Laboratory (INL) that shows how power flows from a hydropower dam, through battery storage, to loads like a data center. I lead the design work and serve as team treasurer, managing our $5,000 budget.

The brief

INL wants a portable display that explains how hydropower and battery storage work together. It has to be quick to set up and airline-friendly, look good, show how power flows through the system, and include recognizable scale replicas of real components like the dam and a data center.

Generating concepts

We are still generating and comparing concepts, and we are evaluating them against a requirement matrix built from those goals. The images below show the direction we are working toward so far, not a final design.

Early concept images

Early mock-up and component models

Alongside the concepts, we built an early physical mock-up to try out layouts, and we started modeling components the display will likely need: a containerized battery unit in CAD and a 3D-printed dam with a removable top and tubing routed through its body.

My role

As design lead, I guide the team’s design direction as we generate and evaluate concepts. As treasurer, I manage our $5,000 budget: I purchase parts and materials, track spending, and keep the project’s financial records.

What’s next

Finish generating concepts, choose a design using the requirement matrix, and then move into detailed design and building the display.
