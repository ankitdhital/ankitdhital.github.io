Topic Overview

This study covers the mechanical design and evaluation of an electronic fin concept — examining geometry, material selection, and integration constraints relevant to fin-based electronic systems.

Objective

Develop and document a fin design that meets both structural and thermal/electronic performance requirements, using engineering analysis to support design decisions.

Method

The study applies mechanical design principles to evaluate fin geometry and configuration against performance criteria, drawing on coursework in materials and design methodology.

Key Insight

Designing fins for electronic applications requires balancing competing constraints — surface area for thermal management, structural rigidity for mounting, and form factor for integration — making it a useful exercise in constraint-driven mechanical design.
