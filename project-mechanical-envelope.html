Why I built it

Engineers are creative. Instead of sending my girlfriend a love letter the old, boring way, I designed an envelope that doubles as a frame. Two rack-and-pinion gears open a heart-shaped face, and three internal slots hold letter-size paper, like a letter or photos.

I printed it in PETG for durability. It made her feel special, and I got to build it doing something I enjoy.
