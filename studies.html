ME-113 · Thermodynamics · May 2025

Waste Heat Recovery from Industry

A thermodynamics review of industrial waste heat recovery across cement, steel, and food sectors. Compares heat exchangers, Organic Rankine Cycle systems, and regenerators against real case data — including a cement plant meeting 30% of its electricity need from kiln exhaust.

ME 111 · Fluid Dynamics · April 2025

Fluid Dynamics Experiment: Water Flow from Jar to Straw

An experimental analysis of water draining from a sealed jar through a glass straw. Measured a flow rate of 1.67×10⁻⁵ m³/s and a Reynolds number of 2828 — transitional flow behaving close to laminar — using Bernoulli and Reynolds relations.
