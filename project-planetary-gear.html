Planetary Gear System

A complete SolidWorks planetary gearbox built from individual gear geometry to working physical assembly and exploded-view documentation.

SolidWorksGears3D printingLaser cutting
Ratio4:1
Sun / Planet / Ring12T · 12T · 36T
WorkflowCAD to assembly

Problem

Translate gear-train theory into a working mechanism where every tooth mesh, carrier, and fastener clearance matters.

Constraint

Planetary gears are compact and unforgiving; small clearance errors create binding during assembly.

Intervention

Modeled the full 3-planet system, fabricated the gears and carrier, assembled the gearbox, and corrected hardware clearance issues discovered in the physical build.

Verification

Compared physical output behavior against the calculated 4:1 ratio and documented the system with final assembly and exploded views.

Photos, CAD, and build artifacts.
