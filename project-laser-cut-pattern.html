Case 07 · CAD · Laser Cutting

Laser-Cut Steel Tiger

A detailed tiger profile converted from image reference to SolidWorks geometry, laser-cut from steel, and hand-finished where the laser could not hold the detail cleanly.

SolidWorksLaser cuttingSteelFinishing
MaterialSteel
Thickness~1/4 in
ToolSolidWorks
FinishLaser + hand work
Problem

Make a visual pattern manufacturable in thick steel without losing the character of the original reference.

Constraint

Digital detail is not automatically cuttable; material thickness and laser limitations decide what survives fabrication.

Intervention

Traced the geometry in SolidWorks, prepared the cut path, laser cut the steel, and hand-finished fine detail.

Verification

Produced a finished steel piece that preserved the visual intent while respecting tool limitations.
