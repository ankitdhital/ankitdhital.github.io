Case 05 · Sheet Metal · Assembly

Mailbox Fabrication

A functional aluminum mailbox fabricated from flat sheet stock, formed to shape, fastened, and mounted on a wooden stand.

Sheet metalBendingFasteningFabrication
MaterialAluminum sheet
ProcessCut · bend · fasten
Tolerance±1/32 in
OutputWorking assembly
Problem

Build a finished physical object where bends, holes, fastening, and final alignment all depend on the order of operations.

Constraint

Sheet metal carries mistakes forward; a misplaced bend affects every later feature.

Intervention

Laid out the pattern, cut, bent, drilled, fastened, and assembled the mailbox before building the stand.

Verification

Completed a working mailbox with a finished assembly tolerance target of ±1/32 inch.
