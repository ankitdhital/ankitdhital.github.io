How I work

Testing gives me the next design decision.

Building and testing show me where a design needs work. These are a few lessons I’ve carried into the next project.

01

Check what the sensor is actually reading.

Thermogate’s thermistor was picking up heat inside its enclosure. Moving it outboard helped it measure the surrounding air.

02

Build early enough to find the fit problems.

My gearbox looked ready in CAD, but the screw heads interfered during assembly. A countersunk chamfer gave them the clearance they needed.

03

Compare the analysis with the hardware.

For the rat trap actuator, I used static FEA to evaluate loading, then cycled the mechanism more than 80 times to check its behavior.

04

Leave useful documentation behind.

During my internship, I wrote procedures that made machine-floor knowledge easier for the next person to use.
